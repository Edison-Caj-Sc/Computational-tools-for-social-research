Computational Tools for Social Research

A collection of personal work developed during my research, shared in the hope that it proves useful for other projects. All tools are open access and may be reused, provided that authorship is acknowledged.

Status: preliminary version. The repository is under construction and its contents, structure and documentation will change as new material is added.

Purpose

This repository gathers computational tools, scripts and workflows built for quantitative social research. The goal is to make them transparent, reusable and easy to adapt, so that other researchers can inspect, replicate or extend them.
